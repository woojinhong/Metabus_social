# Decision Log

## Decision record format

### Decision ID

D-###

### Date

YYYY-MM-DD

### Status

proposed | approved | superseded | rejected

### Decision

_What was decided or proposed._

### Alternatives considered

_Realistic alternatives evaluated._

### Rationale

_Why this option was selected or proposed._

### Evidence

_Facts, research, tests, or other supporting evidence._

### Consequences

_Expected benefits, costs, constraints, and follow-up work._

### Approved by

_User identity or Not approved._
