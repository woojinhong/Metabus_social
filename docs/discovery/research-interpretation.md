# Research Findings and Candidates

## External evidence boundary

Detailed competitor and technology evidence remains under [research](../research/README.md). This document records only project-specific interpretations and unresolved implications.

## Dating-product interpretation

- **Research finding:** operating profile-based products show that profile discovery, mutual matching, subscriptions, and safety tooling can operate at scale.
- **Research finding:** facilitated products show that scheduling, shared prompts, attendance coordination, and optional offline progression are viable interaction patterns in some contexts.
- **Research finding:** discontinued experiments were affected by different strategic contexts; shutdown does not prove a modality or concept inherently invalid.
- **Research interpretation:** scheduled interaction shifts risk toward cohort fill, punctuality, no-shows, facilitation, and operational recovery.
- **Research interpretation:** progressive disclosure may create curiosity but may also postpone and intensify rejection.

See [product-model comparison](../research/dating-products/product-model-comparison.md), [Slide / Single Town](../research/dating-products/slide-single-town-analysis.md), and [Timeleft](../research/dating-products/timeleft-analysis.md).

## Technology interpretation

- **Proposal:** keep session, consent, disclosure, and progression authority in the application domain; treat a media provider as transport.
- **Proposal:** use a time-boxed web-first validation path with explicit real-device failure gates.
- **Proposal:** prefer a modular monolith and relational durability until team or scaling evidence supports extraction.
- **Proposal:** buy managed real-time media for the initial boundary and preserve an adapter and export path.
- **Proposal:** minimize identity and sensitive-profile data; no biometric processing or manual document review.

These are review inputs, not selected technologies. See [technology research](../research/technology/README.md).

## Alternative explanations

Observed shutdown, contraction, or growth may reflect corporate reprioritization, acquisition integration, macroeconomics, marketing, product overlap, operating cost, regulation, liquidity, retention, or several interacting factors. Public evidence rarely isolates a single cause.

Downloads do not prove retention. Revenue does not prove user value. Session duration does not prove relationship formation. Continued operation alone does not prove success.

## Priority evidence gaps

1. Segment-specific problem and willingness-to-attend evidence.
2. Cohort liquidity in a broad Seoul activity area.
3. Emotional response to delayed photo disclosure.
4. Voice-first accessibility and evaluation pressure.
5. Korea RTC quality on real devices and networks.
6. Qualified Korean privacy/legal review of adult verification and sensitive dating data.
7. Operating cost and incident rate without a live facilitator.
8. Repeat value after novelty.
