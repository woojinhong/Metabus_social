# User-Needs Framework

## Purpose

These are product-discovery hypotheses, not validated requirements. Segment differences and accessibility impacts require direct research.

## Functional needs

- **Assumption:** participants need a clear reserved time, eligibility rules, and confidence that the cohort is real enough to attend.
- **Assumption:** participants need device readiness, simple entry, visible session progress, and recovery after interruption.
- **Assumption:** participants need shared material that helps conversation start and continue without demanding clever answers.
- **Assumption:** participants need private interest choices and controlled next steps.
- **Assumption:** operators need cohort, session, safety, cancellation, and support controls.

## Emotional needs

- **Assumption:** participants need lower uncertainty about what to say and what happens next.
- **Assumption:** they need rejection to remain private and non-retaliatory.
- **Assumption:** they need control over photos, video, private contact, and withdrawal.
- **Assumption:** they need permission to pass, answer in text, pause, or leave safely.
- **Risk:** delayed disclosure may increase emotional investment and later disappointment.

## Social needs

- **Assumption:** a bounded group can provide context and social proof without becoming a popularity contest.
- **Assumption:** turn balance, reactions, and prompts can help quieter participants contribute.
- **Risk:** voice, humor, confidence, occupation clues, or dominant participation may concentrate attention even without photos.
- **Boundary:** selection counts and rejection reasons must not be public.

## Trust and safety needs

- **Assumption:** participants need understandable adult/identity checks, while knowing what verification does not prove.
- **Assumption:** account-bound admission, expiring room authorization, blocking, reporting, and moderator response improve willingness to attend.
- **Boundary:** the product cannot promise to prevent local screenshots or recordings.
- **Boundary:** external-contact solicitation, advertising, and follower collection are restricted before compatible mutual progression.
- **Open question:** what evidence and sanctions are proportionate before live validation?

## Identity and self-expression needs

- **Assumption:** broad descriptors and voluntary clues may let participants express themselves without exposing exact personal details.
- **Assumption:** voice, stories, selected non-face media, and behavior can provide useful—but incomplete—signals.
- **Risk:** voice-first interaction can disadvantage hearing- or speech-impaired people, neurodivergent people, non-native speakers, and socially anxious participants.
- **Proposal:** provide pass, text, preparation time, captions where feasible, and equivalent non-media paths.

## Relationship and progression needs

- **Assumption:** dating intent needs explicit boundaries; friendship and romance options may create ambiguity.
- **Proposal:** a participant can privately choose romance, friendship curiosity, further conversation, or no connection.
- **Proposal:** only compatible mutual choices create eligibility for one-to-one voice; they do not imply messaging, webcam, or offline consent.
- **Open question:** whether friendship and romantic intent should coexist in one cohort.

## Reasons to return

- **Assumption:** fresh people, rotating reviewed content, credible attendance, safe handling of rejection, and visible improvement in conversational comfort may support repeat use.
- **Evidence gap:** novelty can be mistaken for durable value; enjoyment, progression, safety, and healthy exit must be measured separately.

## Validation signals

| Need family | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Conversation | participation balance, pass rate, follow-up turns, perceived artificiality |
| Safety | incidents, reporting confidence, retaliation, unwanted contact, withdrawal success |
| Accessibility | task completion and pressure by accommodation need and device |
| Progression | curiosity, compatible mutual choice, later consent, healthy no-selection |
| Repeat value | return intent and actual repeat attendance after novelty |
