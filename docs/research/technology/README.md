## Evidence rules

- Official documentation, pricing, release notes, standards and Korean primary guidance take precedence.
- Option-document recommendations remain research interpretation even when a later decision adopts one.
- Unknown region, price, availability, contract and legal terms remain evidence gaps rather than estimates.
- Each source ledger records source, date, verification date, supported claim and limitation.
