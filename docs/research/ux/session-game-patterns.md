# Session Game and Content Patterns

## Stable-format evaluation

| Candidate format | Curiosity signal | Accessibility and pressure | Privacy/moderation | Repeat use | Proposal assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anonymous choice and guess | Links a response to later identity | Offer pass and text response; avoid clever-answer demand | Sensitive prompts need review | High with rotating packs | First-session candidate |
| Clue-owner matching | Connects media/story to a person | Advance submission can burden users | Strip EXIF; no face requirement | High with consent | First-session candidate |
| Cooperative scenario | Reveals tradeoffs/behavior | Dominant speakers can take over | Avoid sensitive or discriminatory scenarios | Moderate/high | First-session candidate |
| Dating twenty questions | Direct curiosity | Can feel like interview | Prompt boundaries required | Moderate | Later candidate |
| Behavior dilemma | Reveals values more than self-description | Avoid moral scoring | Sexual, political, and trauma topics excluded | High with editorial review | Later candidate |
| Social deduction | Shared energy and observation | Can heighten performance anxiety/exclusion | Needs anti-bullying moderation | Moderate | Optional, not first session |
| Temporary small-group mission | More speaking turns | More room movement/cognitive load | Stage-transition risk | Moderate | Defer pending session evidence |

## Proposal - unapproved first-session set

1. Anonymous choice and participant guessing.
2. Clue-owner matching with voluntary non-face media.
3. Cooperative scenario.
4. Protected free conversation generated by prior material.

This is a proposal, not an approved game requirement. Pass, text response, short thinking time, and clear turn affordances are required research hypotheses, not demonstrated outcomes.

## Content architecture proposal - unapproved

Each item should carry: content ID, format, stage, theme, disclosure level, intimacy, thinking/speaking difficulty, duration, answer mode, relationship signal, safety sensitivity, cultural relevance, season, trend validity, prohibited segments, accessibility note, moderation status, version, and experiment ID. Stable rules can use rotating reviewed content packs.

## Korean trend safety flow

1. Collect candidate cultural or seasonal topics. 2. Exclude politics, crime, death, hate, explicit sexual content, financial solicitation, and active controversy. 3. Human editorial review. 4. Convert to evergreen behavioral scenario. 5. Publish versioned pack. 6. Measure pass rate, delay, follow-up, discomfort reports, and session outcomes. 7. Retire risky or low-quality items.

Research finding: unreviewed trending-search material is unsuitable as direct live-session input because relevance and safety can change faster than session moderation.

## Evaluation and measurement proposal - unapproved

| Metric | What it can indicate | What it cannot prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pass rate | Prompt difficulty or discomfort signal | Compatibility or relationship formation |
| Response delay | Cognitive load or connection issue | Honesty or interest |
| Turn distribution | Participation concentration | Participant value or popularity |
| Follow-up conversation | Topic-generating potential | Safe/private progression quality |
| Discomfort reports | Safety or content concern | Full prevalence without reporting trust |
| Return intent | Session perceived value | Long-term retention |

## Design guardrails

- Do not score participants for attractiveness, humor, confidence, or answers.
- Do not treat face disclosure as a prize, reward, or forced unlock.
- Allow voluntary disclosure and an understandable pass route; never make silence evidence of low interest.
- Use content packs with versioning so a reported prompt can be traced, paused, and retired.
- Test smaller groups and social deduction only after the base voice experience has safety and accessibility evidence.
