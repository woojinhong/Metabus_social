# UX Research Index

## Purpose

Research finding: this library records external interaction patterns and their limits. It does not validate the proposed product, define UI, or approve a participant experience.

## Documents

1. [Competitive patterns](competitive-patterns.md)
2. [Korean user expectations](korean-user-expectations.md)
3. [Session-game patterns](session-game-patterns.md)

## Classification rules

- Research finding: a documented product mechanism or source-backed constraint.
- Proposal: a project-specific adaptation awaiting approval and participant validation.
- Assumption: an expected outcome that needs research or testing.
- Qualitative signal: user-community or review observations; never representative fact.

## Reuse boundary

- Reuse interaction principles, not brands, visual identity, copyrighted artwork, proprietary prompt sets, or confusingly similar trade dress.
- Distinguish reusable patterns from scale-dependent mechanisms, product-specific mechanisms, unsuitable mechanisms, and unresolved hypotheses.

## Major evidence limits

Evidence gap: public marketing pages show product claims, not retention, safety outcomes, or causal UX effectiveness. Korean accessibility, cultural fit, and consent comprehension must be tested with intended participants.
