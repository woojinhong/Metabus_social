# Research

Research supports decisions but does not make them.

## Rules

- Prefer primary and official sources.
- Distinguish confirmed fact, research finding, interpretation, proposal, hypothesis, and evidence gap.
- Include source title, publisher, URL, publication/update date when available, verification date, supported claim, and limitation.
- Record current version or pricing date when relevant.
- Treat quote-only or unpublished prices as evidence gaps.
- Do not select a product, API, technology, or vendor automatically.
- Keep recommendations Proposal—Unapproved until explicit approval is recorded.

## Collections

- [Dating and social-connection products](dating-products/README.md): discontinued, scaled-back, operating, durable, and facilitated models.
- [Technology and vendor options](technology/README.md): delivery, backend, media, data, identity, payment, moderation, notification, hosting, and observability.
- [UX patterns](ux/README.md): competitive patterns, Korean expectations, and session-game patterns.

## Evidence reuse

Specifications link to research but own project behavior proposals. Architecture documents link to research but own technical interpretation. The [discovery interpretation](../discovery/research-interpretation.md) records implications without copying full evidence.

## Verification date

The current collections were verified on 2026-07-27. Current status, capabilities, versions, and prices must be rechecked at a decision gate.
