# Spotify Greenroom / Spotify Live

#### Product status

- **Confirmed fact:** Spotify sunset the standalone live-audio app in 2023 while continuing to explore selected live formats inside Spotify.

#### Proposition and model

Anyone could host or join interest-based live audio rooms, select speakers, use room chat, moderate participation, record conversations, and discover scheduled rooms. A creator fund was announced as a monetization path.

#### Traction and explanation

Spotify cited its overall listener base when integrating Live into the main app, but that number was not Spotify Live usage. It disclosed no Live retention, room attendance, or creator-income metrics. Spotify said the standalone app no longer made sense after experimentation and identified artist listening parties as a more promising in-product format.

#### Product-limit hypothesis

A separate app may have fragmented supply from Spotify's established audience. Spotify did not publish a controlled comparison, so this remains a hypothesis.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
