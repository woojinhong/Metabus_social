# Noondate

#### Current positioning and target

Noondate is a Korean dating and matchmaking service centered on profile cards, relationship intent, and recurring introductions.

#### Discovery and liquidity

Users evaluate cards and profiles and may enter chat after a connection. The official FAQ says the service prioritizes same-age and same-region matches but may relax geography or settings when supply is insufficient. This is direct evidence of a cold-start and segment-liquidity tradeoff.

#### Retention, trust, and monetization

Recurring cards, surveys, community surfaces, virtual Candy, and passes create repeat reasons and paid unlocks. Clear-face photos, profile review, acquaintance blocking, reporting, permanent bans, and re-registration blocking are documented. July 2026 moderation and service notices verify continued operation.

#### Durability and limits

The service dates to 2011/2012 and shows a live cumulative match count above 71m. Longevity and current notices support durability; cumulative matches do not reveal current active users, conversation quality, or relationships.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
