# Discontinued Dating and Social-Connection Products

## Scope and inclusion

A product appears here only when a primary source, official notice, filing, or reputable report carrying a company statement verifies a shutdown, completed pilot, feature retirement, or material scale-back. These outcomes do not prove the original interaction model was invalid.

## Cross-product patterns

- **Research finding:** documented causes vary: portfolio allocation, strategic fit, product-surface consolidation, and uncertainty about an emerging format.
- **Research finding:** large platforms can retire an experiment despite the underlying modality continuing elsewhere.
- **Research finding:** company disclosures rarely publish product-level retention, liquidity, incident, or unit-economics evidence.
- **Research interpretation:** a separate app or scheduled format may increase supply fragmentation, but the public record does not isolate this as a universal cause.
- **Research interpretation:** privacy and coordination friction are useful risks to test when they appear in documented launch context; they are not proven shutdown causes unless a source says so.

## Patterns that remain unproven

The evidence does not support universal claims that:

- metaverse or avatar dating is inherently unsuccessful;
- group interaction lacks demand;
- synchronous products necessarily fail on liquidity;
- moderation or gender imbalance caused these shutdowns;
- monetization or product-market fit was the primary cause;
- shutdown invalidates the original user need.

Alternative explanations include acquisition integration, corporate reprioritization, strategic overlap, macroeconomics, infrastructure cost, regulatory or safety burden, weak retention, weak monetization, inadequate liquidity, and interacting causes.

## Implications for future product research

- **Proposal:** test cohort fill, attendance, repeat use, disclosure comfort, and safe rejection independently.
- **Proposal:** preserve an exit from vendor-specific or standalone-surface assumptions.
- **Open question:** can a scheduled product concentrate demand enough to offset synchronization friction?
- **Open question:** does progressive disclosure improve curiosity without intensifying delayed rejection?

These are research prompts, not approved project requirements.

## Evidence gaps

No reviewed source provides comparable cohort retention, completed-connection outcomes, incident rates, local liquidity, or full unit economics across these products. Slide / Single Town lacks a primary final-shutdown notice and remains classified only as materially scaled back.
