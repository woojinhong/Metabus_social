# Tinder Social

#### Product status

- **Confirmed fact:** Tinder's own launch page now records that Tinder Social was discontinued in August 2017.

#### Original product proposition and interaction model

Friends formed groups, swiped on nearby groups, entered a group chat after a mutual match, and planned an in-person activity. The product was location-proximate, same-night oriented, group-to-group, and open to friendship or romantic connection.

#### Safety and privacy

The broader launch required users to unlock Social before friends could see them. The earlier Australian test generated documented privacy concern because dating-app participation could be exposed to Facebook friends. That concern is confirmed; it is not a confirmed shutdown cause.

#### Traction, monetization, and retention

A Tinder spokesperson called adoption modest despite no real marketing effort. No count, denominator, retention, conversion, revenue, moderation, or incident measure was published.

#### Official explanation

Tinder said the initial format did not fit its future direction, then focused on video, location, and AI-driven features. This is stronger evidence than external speculation about privacy or group liquidity.

#### Product-limit hypotheses

- **Plausible hypothesis:** coordinating several friends and another group increased activation friction.
- **Plausible hypothesis:** friend-graph visibility increased privacy cost.
- **Evidence gap:** neither is established as a causal factor.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
