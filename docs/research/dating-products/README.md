# Dating-Product Research

## Purpose

This set compares external dating, social-discovery, voice, group, and facilitated-connection products. It informs questions and proposals; it does not decide this project's direction.

## Inclusion and classification

- Discontinued analysis requires verified shutdown, feature retirement, completed pilot, or material scale-back.
- Operating status requires a recent official product or company surface.
- Durability requires evidence beyond existence, such as revenue, payers, longevity, expansion, repeat events, or sustained investment.
- Unclear products remain uncertain rather than forced into a category.

## Source quality

Priority: official product/company sites; help, policy, pricing, and notices; filings; app-store listings; company interviews/blogs; reputable journalism; peer-reviewed and credible market research. Community reviews are qualitative signals only.

Every detailed analysis records source title, publisher, URL, publication/status date, verification date, supported claim, and limitation. Official explanation, business context, reported interpretation, hypothesis, and evidence gap remain distinct.

## Classification limits

Shutdown does not prove conceptual invalidity. Revenue does not prove user value. Downloads do not prove retention. Session duration does not prove relationship formation. A current app may be only an operating comparator when durability evidence is weak.

## Major limitations

Public evidence rarely provides comparable local liquidity, retention, incident, accessibility, relationship-outcome, or unit-economics data. Several scale figures are company-reported and use changing definitions. Current status and evidence were verified on 2026-07-27.
