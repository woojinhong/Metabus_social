# Facebook Campus

#### Product status

- **Confirmed fact:** Meta's product page says the pilot was completed and Campus was no longer offered as of 2022-03-11.

#### Proposition and model

Campus was an opt-in, school-email-verified section of Facebook for classmate discovery, a school directory, school-bounded groups and events, and chat. It was always-on, identity-scoped, group and event oriented, and supported asynchronous and synchronous coordination.

#### Trust, monetization, and scale

School-email verification limited membership. Users could block people and delete profiles. Meta said Campus activity could inform personalization, including advertising. The shutdown reporting identified 204 pilot schools, but no active-user or retention measure.

#### Official explanation

Meta said it learned that Facebook Groups was one of the most effective ways to support students. This supports a product-surface consolidation interpretation, but the evidence does not establish that Campus was absorbed into Groups or that college social connection lacked demand.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
