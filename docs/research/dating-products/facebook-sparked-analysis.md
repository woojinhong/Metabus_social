# Facebook Sparked

#### Product status

- **Confirmed fact:** Meta closed the external beta on 2022-01-20 and offered testers a data export before deleting accounts.

#### Original product proposition

Sparked attempted to recreate speed-dating serendipity through an experience described as rooted in kindness.

#### Interaction model

Users joined scheduled local or demographic events, rotated through four-minute video dates, could mutually continue for ten minutes, and could then share phone, email, or Instagram details. Meta also tested audio-only events. The service was synchronous, scheduled, one-to-one, and event-batched.

#### Safety, conversation, retention, and monetization

The short timed conversation was the initiation scaffold. Public sources reviewed do not establish identity checks, moderation operations, reporting, unwanted-contact controls after information exchange, pricing, repeat attendance, or retention.

#### Shutdown timeline and explanation

Meta's tester email said the team improved, learned, and made connections, but that Sparked had to end. A spokesperson provided no additional reason. Reporting interpreted the wording as a lack of traction, but Meta did not publish a causal metric.

#### Product-limit hypotheses

- **Plausible hypothesis:** scheduled synchronous video required sufficient local supply at the same time and imposed higher participation pressure than asynchronous browsing.
- **Evidence gap:** no attendance, match, continuation, or repeat-event figures are public.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
