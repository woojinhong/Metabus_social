# MUNTO

#### Current positioning and target

MUNTO is an interest-based online/offline community spanning one-time Socialing events, ongoing Clubs, Challenges, and a feed-like Lounge. It targets friendship, activity, networking, language exchange, and other non-exclusive connection goals rather than explicit dating.

#### Liquidity and retention

The product can move a user from a single event into a repeated group, host follow, feed, or challenge. Company pages report roughly 15k new communities monthly and more than 1m signups. A 2025 host newsletter said member demand exceeded Socialing supply in several categories, showing that hosts, events, and venues—not just participant registrations—are the liquidity constraint.

#### Trust, monetization, and burden

Profiles, manner scores, reviews, organizer rules, and certified places provide trust signals. Consumer virtual currency and host/venue promotion provide monetization surfaces. Experience quality and safety depend substantially on distributed hosts, event supply, and venues, raising training, enforcement, and support requirements.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
