# Meetup

#### Current positioning and target

Meetup lets organizers create groups and events around interests while members discover local communities. It supports friendship, activity, professional, identity, and mixed social purposes but does not make romance the default intent.

#### Discovery, liquidity, and retention

Interest and location lead to groups; repeated events and persistent communities create continuity. Cold start is organizer-side as well as member-side: useful supply requires a credible host, a recurring calendar, and local attendees.

#### Trust, monetization, and durability

Organizers historically paid subscriptions, and the current owner introduced a free organizer plan while retaining paid Standard and Pro tiers. Meetup was acquired by Bending Spoons in January 2024; the owner announced nearly $50m of planned product and membership investment and now reports redesigned apps and a free organizer plan. This supports continued investment and long operating history, while product-level active attendance, organizer retention, and Meetup-specific revenue remain unavailable.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
