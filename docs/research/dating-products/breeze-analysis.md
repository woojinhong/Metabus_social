# Breeze

#### Current operating status and user problem

Breeze remains live as of a 2026-07-13 Google Play update. It removes ordinary pre-date chat and sells an arranged date after a mutual match.

#### Interaction, liquidity, and retention

Users receive a bounded daily selection. A match moves to shared availability and a partner venue. The model concentrates commitment but depends on local compatible supply, venue coverage, cancellation handling, and both participants' availability. Public repeat-date and completed-date rates were not found.

#### Trust and safety

Breeze says users are screened or verified, dates occur at partner venues, staff are present, and no-shows can trigger account freezes. These are developer claims without an independent effectiveness audit.

#### Monetization and durability

There are no ads or general subscriptions; both users pay for a scheduled date. This aligns revenue with offline progression but adds refund, venue, no-show, and safety burdens. A current listing and 1m+ Android downloads support operation, not proven commercial durability.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
