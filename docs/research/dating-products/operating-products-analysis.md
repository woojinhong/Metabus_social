# Operating and Demonstrably Durable Dating and Social-Connection Products

## Definition

Operating status requires a current official product, help, policy, app-store, release, or investor surface. Durability requires additional evidence such as revenue, payers, longevity, expansion, repeated-event activity, or sustained investment. Brand awareness, downloads, and continued existence alone are insufficient.

## Cross-product patterns

- **Research finding:** large profile apps combine broad supply, reciprocal matching, recurring discovery, paid visibility/subscription, and substantial trust-and-safety investment.
- **Research finding:** facilitated products concentrate intent around a scheduled event and carry attendance, venue, host, cancellation, and support burdens.
- **Research finding:** interest communities rely on organizer or event supply as well as participant demand.
- **Research interpretation:** a bounded daily or scheduled inventory can reduce browsing but increases the cost of poor local supply.
- **Research interpretation:** prompts, shared activities, and structured progression can reduce blank-page friction; they do not prove lower pressure or better relationships.

## Common mechanisms

| Mechanism | Evidence boundary | Transferability question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reciprocal interest | common in dating products | what compatible intent is required before private access? |
| Prompts/shared context | current product feature | does it create voluntary continuation here? |
| Bounded inventory | daily cards or scheduled cohorts | can local supply remain relevant and fair? |
| Identity/safety tooling | sustained investment | which controls are proportionate and effective? |
| Event commitment | paid or coordinated attendance | can attendance improve without payment? |
| Repeated community | events, hosts, content | can the product create repeat value after novelty? |

## Scale-dependent mechanisms

Recommendation quality, local choice, fraud signals, always-available support, and rapid replacement depend on network scale, brand, data, and operating investment. These mechanisms should not be assumed available to a new product.

## Operationally expensive mechanisms

Facilitators, venues, identity review, moderation, refunds, incident handling, event supply, and customer support add human and vendor cost. Revenue does not show that these costs are economical for this project.

## Evidence gaps

Comparable active users, local fill, cohort retention, completed dates, relationship outcomes, incident rates, accessibility outcomes, and unit economics are not public for most products. Company-reported scale is labeled and not treated as audited retention.
