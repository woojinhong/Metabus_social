# WIPPY

#### Current positioning and target

WIPPY combines local friendship, activity companions, entertainment, and dating. This is an always-on, mixed-intent social-discovery model.

#### Discovery, liquidity, and retention

Profiles, local recommendations, voice features, first-impression evaluation, games, and in-app chat provide repeated discovery. The company reports 10m+ downloads, 8m+ signups, and a large cumulative match count, but does not disclose current MAU, local supply balance, or cohort retention.

#### Trust and safety

Official materials describe PASS identity checks, selfie verification, human profile review, AI abnormal-behavior detection, reporting and blocking, 24-hour monitoring, and blocking of links or malicious files. The company also warns about romance scams and off-platform contact, demonstrating an ongoing rather than solved risk.

#### Monetization and limits

Free access coexists with virtual currency and a short-duration WIPPY Pass for discovery and contact benefits. Company-reported scale and current releases support operation and market presence; they do not establish user value, response quality, or safety effectiveness.

## Evidence boundary

**Research finding:** operating status or closure is verified as stated. Product causality, retention, relationship outcomes, and transferability remain limited to the cited evidence.
