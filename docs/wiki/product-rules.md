# Product Rules Summary

- Seoul, ages 25-39, 19+ on participation date, mutually compatible heterosexual Pilot cohort.
- Exactly six, target three women/three men; no silent compatibility relaxation.
- Ninety-minute group voice: three games plus free conversation, then private interest/reveal/final choice.
- Initial interest zero-to-two; reveal only on mutual initial interest plus subject consent; final choice zero-to-one.
- Final compatible mutual choice grants ten-minute pair voice only.
- No public counts/rejection reasons, group webcam, private text, payment, recording, external contact, biometric/doc review or popularity scoring.
